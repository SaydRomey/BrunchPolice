
# LGBTQ+ Inclusion Ideas

Brainstorm on incorporating subtle LGBTQ+ activism into the game.  
Could be done thoughtfully by normalizing representation and creating an inclusive environment.  
Here are some ideas for Brunch Police that align with this vision:

---

## Character Representation

### Diverse NPCs
Include NPCs with a variety of gender expressions, body types, and clothing styles that reflect the LGBTQ+ community.

Some couples dining together could be same-sex or non-binary pairings, represented as part of the normal brunch crowd.

### Player Customization:
Allow the player to choose their character's appearance, including non-binary options, 
with hairstyles, clothing, and pronouns that reflect diversity.

### Boss Backstories:
Integrate LGBTQ+ aspects subtly into the characters.  
For example, a boss like **Julie(*Jordan*) "Juice Jacker"** might have a dialogue or setting 
that hints at her sharing juice with her wife or partner.

---

## Environmental Storytelling

### Decor and Signs:
Display pride flags or subtle rainbow decor in some areas, 
like on napkins, a painting in the brunch area, 
or small signs of inclusivity like **“All Are Welcome”** in the restaurant.

### Menus or Items:
Include themed items like *“Rainbow Pancakes”* or *“Pride Smoothie”* as subtle nods to LGBTQ+ culture.

### Couples' Photos:
In isometric or background art, include framed photos of diverse couples in the restaurant.

---

## Dialogue and Interactions

### Casual Mentions:
Some NPCs could mention their partners in casual conversation:  
> “My husband loves these croissants!”  
> “My girlfriend and I always share the syrup pancakes.”


### Mini-Quests:
Include a side quest where a customer asks for help planning a brunch proposal for their same-sex partner, 
or finding a lost item they got from their significant other.

---

## Gameplay and Themes

### Rainbow-Themed Power-Ups:
Some levels could feature “Rainbow Boosts” or similar mechanics that subtly hint at LGBTQ+ positivity.

### Flags in Levels:
Each level could feature environmental storytelling with subtle nods to LGBTQ+ activism. 
For example:
  - A flag in Grease Canyon could have a bacon design but with a rainbow tint.
  - In Candy Chaos, a pride-themed cupcake power-up could appear.

---

## Celebratory Events or Achievements

### In-Game Celebrations:
A special brunch event in the game could honor pride with subtle rainbow decorations 
and unique LGBTQ+ themed interactions.

### Achievements:
Use affirming titles for achievements like *“Everyone’s Welcome!”* 
for completing a level with inclusivity-themed nods.

---

## Subtle Visual Details

### Clothing Choices:
Some characters could wear pins, hats, or subtle patterns that nod to LGBTQ+ flags 
(e.g., rainbow, trans, bi, or asexual colors).

### Level-Specific Details:
Sticky Syrup Swamp could have syrup bottles with small rainbow labels.  
Citrus Cascade could include Pride-colored citrus decorations.

---

## Community Focus

### Credits and Thanks:
In the credits, we could acknowledge LGBTQ+ communities and allies 
for their contributions to diversity and creativity in gaming.

### Charitable Efforts:
Mention subtle in-game text that says part of the proceeds of a certain “Brunch Pass” or DLC 
could support LGBTQ+ charities.

---

These elements should be woven organically into the game to create a world that feels inclusive 
without it being performative or forced.
