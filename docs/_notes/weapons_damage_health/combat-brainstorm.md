## Enemy categories for level of effect of weapons

ex. with bacon gun (throws a slice of bacon doing damage and wrapping
target in bacon, immobilizing the enemy):

- A small enemy would get killed.

- A medium enemy would receive damage and be wrapped (or killed if
damage is critical).

-A large enemy or mob enemy (like bats or bees) would receive damage
only.

- Aboss might be immune or receive damage.
