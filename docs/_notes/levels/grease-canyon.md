### Grease Canyon

<!-- <img src="./assets/grease-canyon.png" alt="Grease Canyon Preview" width="200"> -->

#### **Level Elements**:
Platforms made of sizzling bacon strips.  
Pits of boiling grease bubbling below.  
Ropes made of hanging sausage links.  

#### **Background**:
Details like sizzling griddles or fat clouds

#### **Color Palette**:
Warm reds, oranges, golds, with breakfast-food textures

#### **Enemies**:
Small angry pigs trying to knock you off platforms.  
Worm-like bacon strips moving up and down ropes.  
Flying sausage links that swoop down at you.

#### **Boss Fight**:
Barry uses a "Bacon Tornado" attack, 
spinning wildly with bacon flying everywhere.  

Avoid his attacks by jumping between platforms 
and hitting him with your Bacon Gun when he slows down.

#### **Power-ups**:
Grease Slide: Dash to avoid the tornado.  
Extra Bacon: Absorbs a hit from the flying bacon debris.

#### **Evidence to Collect**:
Bacon Strips

---

🧱 Platforms:
- Platforms are sizzling, animated bacon strips in warm reds and browns.
- Fat drips off edges with a looping pixel shimmer effect.
- Slight wiggle animation to show they’re hot and greasy.

🔥 Hazards:
- Boiling grease pits with bubbling, golden-yellow animations and occasional splashes.
- Dripping grease from ceilings and edges.

🪢 Ropes and Ladders:
- Sausage-link ropes, animated to sway slightly.
- Ropes attach to bacon ceilings with glistening fat at the joints.

☁️ Background:
- Pixel clouds shaped like bacon grease blobs.
- Sizzling griddles and huge cracked eggs float in the sky.
- Occasional bacon comets zip by for parallax effect.

🐖 Enemies:
- 🐷 Small angry pigs with bacon armor — animated to charge and push.
- 🪱 Bacon worms slithering up/down sausage ropes.
- 🌭 Flying sausage links that dive at you in pixel flapping loops.

💥 Boss Area:
- Center platform, wider with bacon spiral columns and a bacon tornado FX loop.
- Barry “Bacon Bandit” wears pixel shades, an apron, and has a bacon-whip arm.
- Animated “Bacon Tornado” — a swirl of bacon frames cycling rapidly.

🧰 Power-ups:
- Grease Slide: Pixel sprite with sparkles and a quick-dash streak.
- Extra Bacon: Golden bacon shield hovering around the player sprite.

🔍 Collectibles:
- Animated bacon strips — glimmering slightly when untouched.
